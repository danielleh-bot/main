What to check before shipping this kit

Body copy and section headings are the same style

article_body and section_headings both resolve to Open Sans 16px / 600 / 25.6px. A 600-weight running text is unusual, so the extractor probably sampled a teaser or card, not article prose. Verify against a second article before trusting it.

The display face rests on a single observation

Signika Negative has usage_count: 1 against Open Sans’ 909 — the whole hero treatment hangs on one measured element. Confident enough to preview, thin enough to re-check.

Both gaps land on the recommendation card

The only two substituted tokens are article_title_card and article_lead. Card titles are the single most visible text in a widget, so a headline coverage number of 89% understates the risk here.

Unstyled links are in the colour sample

#0000EE appears on 30 elements — that is the browser default, not a brand decision. Worth excluding from the palette so it never reaches a widget.

Zero radius is a real signal

Buttons and thumbnails both measure 0px. Squared corners are load-bearing for this brand, so a rounded default widget reads as foreign — switch to No kit above to see how much.

Header contrast passes

#FFFFFF on #BE1F24 clears 4.5:1, so the extracted header.is_dark flag and its light-text pairing can be applied without a manual override.
